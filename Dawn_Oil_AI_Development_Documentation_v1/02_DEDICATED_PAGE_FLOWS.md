## Supporting flows
- Director biography `/leadership/[slug]`: only approved slug, full name, role, photo and biography, breadcrumb back to Leadership. No blank biography shells.
- Privacy Notice `/privacy`: accurate handling of actual personal data and providers, approved before release.
- Website Terms `/terms`: company-reviewed legal wording; don't auto-create unsupported legal commitments.
- `/404`: polite not-found message with Home and Contact links.
- Future Assets/Projects, News, Company Profile download: **deferred and not exposed** until management provides evidence and approves content.
- Enquiry: user selects appropriate contact CTA → Contact page → sees verified channels → uses approved workflow; do not request confidential bid/data-room documents.
