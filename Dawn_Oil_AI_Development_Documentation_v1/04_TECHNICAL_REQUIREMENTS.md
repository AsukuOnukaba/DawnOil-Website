## Architecture — proposed and bounded
- Static site generated by **Astro + TypeScript + CSS**; shared components, no bespoke backend, database, login, content-dashboard or CMS unless explicitly reapproved. This follows the client's recent preference for low management overhead on Aureum and is **recommended for Dawn**, while S1 requests editable content management. Keep this as a **decision to confirm**, not a settled CEO demand for Dawn.
- Static data for 8 pages and published director profiles. Content updates by developer-controlled commits and approved deploys. Avoid unnecessary React/client JS except responsive menu/reduced-motion enhancements.
- Local assets under public images; developer-only original briefs/documents remain outside `/public` and generated output. Do not expose personal/private data in repos or metadata.
- Forms: original S1 requests a working enquiry form. Static output still needs an approved managed form-to-email provider or a verified `mailto` fallback. Do not silently drop the form requirement; document choice and security/retention implications.
- Domain, email and hosting: CEO owns business accounts and final launch approval. Do not invent domain or placeholder email. HTTPS, redirects, canonical, robots and sitemap after final URL approval.
- Security: no secrets in client bundle. Provider env vars in deployment configuration. CSP where compatible, validated inputs, spam controls and privacy notice matching actual workflow.

## Quality
- Responsive ≥320px. Keyboard operable header/footer, WCAG 2.2 AA-oriented contrast and focus, accessible labels, image alt descriptions, no hidden text behind fixed header.
- SEO: unique title/description per page, one H1, correct Open Graph and canonical when domain verified, JSON-LD only for confirmed company identity, sitemap, 404. Never put invented assets/production data in SEO metadata.
- Performance aspirations (not guarantees): compressed WebP/AVIF derivatives where justified; lazy-load below-fold images; hero LCP carefully optimised; respect reduced motion and avoid layout shift.
- Testing: Playwright Firefox 1.38.0 pinned only if compatible on Monterey/Node runtime; smoke test links/menu, console, overflow; visual baselines at approved viewports; build command `npm run build`.
