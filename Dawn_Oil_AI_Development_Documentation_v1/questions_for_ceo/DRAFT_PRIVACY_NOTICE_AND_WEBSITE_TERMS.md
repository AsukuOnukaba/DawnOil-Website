# Dawn Oil Limited — Draft Privacy Notice and Website Terms

Status: **internal management and legal review only — do not publish**. Prepared 1 November 2026 to reflect the current static Astro website. Bracketed decisions must be resolved before approval.

## Architecture reflected in these drafts

- The website is a static informational website.
- The business enquiry form is visible but submission is disabled. It currently sends no personal data to Dawn Oil or a form provider.
- No analytics, advertising trackers, marketing subscriptions, user accounts, CMS, database or file uploads are configured.
- The production domain, hosting provider, public privacy contact and form-delivery provider are not yet approved.
- A hosting provider may process routine technical logs once deployed; the provider, log fields and retention period must be confirmed before publication.
